# Opening Radio Builder on a Mac

The first time, your Mac blocks **Start Radio Builder.command** because it came
from the internet. You only need to allow it once.

1. Double-click **Start Radio Builder.command**. A message says Apple could not
   verify it. Click **Done** (not **Move to Trash**).
2. Open **System Settings** → **Privacy & Security** and scroll down to
   **Security**. Next to "Start Radio Builder.command was blocked", click
   **Open Anyway** and enter your Mac password.
3. Double-click **Start Radio Builder.command** again and click **Open Anyway**.

Radio Builder opens in your browser. From now on, a double-click is enough.

**No Open Anyway button?** It only shows for about an hour after step 1. Do
step 1 again, then go straight to step 2.

**macOS 14 or older:** right-click the file, choose **Open**, then click **Open**.
